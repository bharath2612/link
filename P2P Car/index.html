Brainstorm

Pros

Why people will use it
1

Your car pays for itself

It's losing value sitting in the basement anyway. Rent it out a handful of days a month and that covers the insurance and the Salik, maybe more.

2

You get a proper car, not a tired rental

Cars people actually own and live with tend to be cleaner and looked after better than something a hundred renters have thrashed. And it costs about the same.

3

You can get the car you actually want

Rental fleets stock the safe, boring stuff. Here you might find the right SUV for a desert trip, an EV to try for a week, or something fun for the weekend.

4

It's already where you are

The car's in your building or down the road. No airport counter, no queue, no desk. Book it, unlock it, go.

5

The owner keeps control

They pick the price, set a mileage limit, and decide who they're happy handing the keys to.

Cons

The bits we have to solve
1

Bringing it back on time really matters

With a normal rental, running late just adds an hour to the bill and nobody minds. Here the owner might be waiting outside needing to get to work. That's a real squeeze and we have to plan for it.

2

People go one way, the car has to come back

Most trips aren't a loop. You drive somewhere and stay. But the car still has to end up back in its own spot, and that return leg is enough to put some renters off.

3

Where does the renter actually park it?

Loads of buildings here have assigned bays behind a gate or an RFID barrier. Giving a stranger that access, and making sure the bay is free when they get back, is fiddly.

4

The car ages on someone else's trips

Every kilometre a renter does is wear the owner didn't put on it. We'll need sensible mileage caps and a clear line on what's fair use.

5

Scratches turn into "wasn't me"

Without good before and after photos, a fresh dent or a stained seat ends up as one person's word against the other's.

6

People try things in cars that aren't theirs

Off-roading in the dunes, a blast up Jebel Hafeet, using it as a quiet taxi, or taking it over to Oman without telling anyone.

Still a working list. We'll keep adding the cases that matter as we pressure-test the idea.

How it works

01

Owner lists

Photos, price and availability. We verify and onboard the car for rental.

02

Renter discovers

A map of nearby available cars with live pricing, ratings and distance.

03

Selects time

Pick hours or days. KYC, deposit and insurance applied automatically.

04

Drives

Keyless or smart-key handover. Telematics track location and condition.

05

Returns

Drop back, condition verified by photo, payment settles, owner earns.

    Aged 21 or over — 25+ for premium and luxury vehicles.
  • —
    Residents: valid UAE licence and Emirates ID for KYC.
  • —
    Tourists: home licence (eligible nationalities) or International Driving Permit, plus passport.
  • —
    Licence held at least 12 months; credit card for deposit and fines.
